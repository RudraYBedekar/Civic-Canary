# River County Benefits Assistance Playbook

## Required documents

Ask clients to bring proof of identity and proof of River County residence. Do not tell a client
that they are eligible; program staff must verify requirements against the official portal.

## Language assistance

Offer the official Spanish-language portal link. If it is unavailable, connect the client with a
trained language-assistance staff member instead of improvising a translation.

## Application support

Staff may explain field labels and provide accessibility assistance. They must never enter or
submit personal information on a client's behalf through Civic Canary.

## Eligibility and deadlines

Staff must quote eligibility rules and deadlines from the official portal. Any detected change to
these rules requires a playbook-owner review before guidance is updated.
